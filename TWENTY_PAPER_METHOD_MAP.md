# Twenty-Paper Method Map

The detailed paper-to-Field-10 map is preserved in `FIELD10_20_PAPER_METHOD_MAP.md`. The implementation status must be interpreted as follows:

- **Production reused:** protected existing ranking, exact-symbol evidence, immutable snapshots and current decision formulas.
- **Production repaired:** selector/load identity, child artifact persistence, publication validation diagnostics, provider routing and reload behavior.
- **Shadow/research only:** Hamilton regime support, DCC/dependence, Ledoit–Wolf covariance, HRP-style overlays, calibration/conformal candidates, drift/change detection, candidate utility, learning-to-rank/global forecasting and statistical promotion tests.
- **Not automatically promoted:** White Reality Check, Hansen SPA, PBO, Deflated Sharpe, TFT, DeepAR, temporal relational ranking, FinBERT/event models and fractional-Kelly sizing require their own validated evidence.

The methodological foundations requested are White; Hansen; Bailey et al.; Bailey and López de Prado; Hamilton; Engle; Ledoit–Wolf; López de Prado; DeMiguel et al.; Kelly; Jegadeesh–Titman; Moskowitz–Ooi–Pedersen; Gu–Kelly–Xiu; Feng et al.; Lim et al.; Salinas et al.; Romano–Patterson–Candès; Gibbs–Candès; Bifet–Gavaldà; and Aracı. Their names do not imply that every candidate is production-ready.
