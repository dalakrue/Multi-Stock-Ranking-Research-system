# Rank Stability Report

## Classification: SHADOW_ONLY

Candidate utilities are resampled with a deterministic seed derived from symbols, utilities and model version. Persisted evidence includes probability rank=1, probability rank≤4, median rank, 5th/95th rank percentiles, instability and score gap.

Only eligible candidates receive utility; blocked/insufficient symbols remain displayed. Historical parent ranks are never recomputed. Production stability cannot be evaluated until repeated daily shadow publications exist.
