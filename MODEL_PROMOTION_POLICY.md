# Model Promotion Policy

Research candidates remain `RESEARCH_ONLY`, `SHADOW` or `VALIDATION_PENDING` until they pass preregistered out-of-sample gates. Code existence is not evidence of superiority.

Minimum promotion evidence includes immutable predictions/outcomes, purged walk-forward or embargoed validation, adequate sample counts, transaction-cost-aware utility, calibration and interval coverage, drift stability, rank stability/top-k metrics, drawdown/tail-risk limits, and data-snooping controls such as SPA/Reality Check/PBO/Deflated Sharpe where applicable.

A candidate cannot self-promote. Promotion must be an explicit persisted governance decision with model/feature/formula versions, cutoff dates, evidence hashes and rejection reason. Failed candidates remain shadow/rejected; production history is never rewritten.
